Voicemail capture

Turn homeowner voicemails into organized lead rows automatically.

When a homeowner leaves a voicemail, that job shouldn't sit in your inbox unread. Answered Call pulls the caller name, phone, address, service needed, urgency, and full transcript out of the voicemail and writes a clean row straight to Google Sheets — with the details available in the message.

How it works

Three steps from voicemail to sheet row

No dashboard to log into. No manual data entry. Processed voicemail details are written to Google Sheets, and an alert can be sent to your team afterward.

01
Homeowner leaves a voicemail

You're on a job. The homeowner calls, doesn't reach you, and leaves a message describing their crawlspace water, foundation issue, or other problem.

Voicemail received
02
Fields are extracted automatically

The voicemail transcript is processed and parsed. Caller name, phone number, address, service needed, urgency, and a plain-language summary are pulled out.

No manual entry
03
Row written to Google Sheets

A new row appears in your Voicemail Leads sheet and an alert is sent with the key details — via email or SMS, depending on setup. You know who called, what they need, and how urgent it is before you call back.

What gets captured

Turn voicemails into structured records

The workflow extracts these fields when available in the voicemail and writes the result to your Google Sheet.

Caller Name Extracted from the voicemail greeting
Phone Formatted and ready to tap-to-call
Address Property address mentioned in the voicemail
Service Needed Drainage / water mitigation, foundation repair, crawlspace, or other — normalized automatically
Urgency HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW — flagged from context clues in the voicemail
Summary Plain-language description of the homeowner's problem
Transcript Transcribed voicemail text
Status / Source Automatically set to NEW and Voicemail on capture
